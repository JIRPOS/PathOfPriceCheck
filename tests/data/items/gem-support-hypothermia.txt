Hypothermia Support
--------
Cold, Support
Level: 16
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 130%
--------
Requirements:
Level: 62
Dex: 99 (unmet)
--------
Supports any skill that deals damage.
--------
Supported Skills have +20% chance to Freeze Enemies which are Chilled
(Freeze lowers Enemy Action Speed to zero, preventing them from acting. Duration is based on the Cold Damage of the Hit)
Supported Skills deal 27% more Cold Damage Over Time
Supported Skills deal 27% more Damage with Hits and Ailments against Chilled Enemies
(Ailments that deal Damage are Bleeding, Ignited, and Poisoned)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills have +0(0-20)% chance to Freeze Enemies which are Chilled
(Freeze lowers Enemy Action Speed to zero, preventing them from acting. Duration is based on the Cold Damage of the Hit)
--------
Experience: 1,521,173/15,058,455
--------
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
